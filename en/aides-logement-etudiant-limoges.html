CAF benefits

APL or ALS: what's the difference?

Both benefits are paid by the CAF (the French family-allowance fund) and are often similar in amount. The difference comes down to the legal status of the home — not to you.

APL
Personalised housing benefit
APL-approved homes
Who is eligible? Any student renting an APL-approved home (the landlord has signed an agreement with the State)
Estimated amount €100–200/month depending on income, rent and household
Payment Directly to the tenant (or the landlord, depending on the agreement)
Where to apply? At caf.fr — under "Apply for a benefit"
ALS
Social housing allowance
Non-approved homes
Who is eligible? Any student renting a non-approved home, subject to income
Estimated amount €80–180/month depending on income, rent and household
Payment Directly to the tenant, monthly by bank transfer
Where to apply? At caf.fr — same form and process as APL

Step by step

How do I apply for housing benefit?

The whole process is done online at caf.fr as soon as you've signed your lease. Here are the steps.

1
Sign your tenancy agreement
You can only apply after signing the lease. You'll need your move-in date and the exact rent including charges.
2
Create your online CAF account
Go to caf.fr and create your personal account. You'll need your social-security number, a valid email address and your bank details (RIB).
3
Apply for the benefit
In your CAF account, click "Apply for a benefit". Enter your situation (student, tenant), the details of the home, and declare your income (your tax notice from two years ago, or a nil-income statement if you have none).
4
Attach the supporting documents
The CAF will ask for: your signed lease, your ID, your bank details (RIB), your latest tax notice (or a nil-income statement) and your proof of enrolment at your institution.
5
Wait for the decision and first payment
The CAF usually processes your application in 2 to 4 weeks. Once accepted, the benefit is paid from the month after you moved in. Payment is made on the 5th of each month.
6
The Begip team helps you
The Begip agency helps you put together your CAF application and gather the documents. Feel free to contact the agency by phone on +33 5 55 33 97 50 or by email at agence@begip.fr.
Beyond APL/ALS

Other benefits & schemes for students

APL and ALS aren't the only options. Other schemes can top up your housing budget.

🎓
Mobili-Jeune — Action Logement
A grant of €10–100/month for work-study students (apprenticeship or professionalisation contract) under 30. Can be combined with APL or ALS. Apply directly at actionlogement.fr.
Learn more →
🔐
Visale — Free student guarantor
Visale is a free guarantee from Action Logement that replaces a personal guarantor (parents, relatives). It covers unpaid rent and reassures landlords. Open to all students under 30.
Learn more →
📚
CROUS grants — Housing support
CROUS grant holders receive extra support and can access reduced-rate CROUS university residences. Apply at messervices.etudiant.gouv.fr before 31 May for the following year.
Learn more →
💶
LOCA-PASS advance
Action Logement
An interest-free loan to fund your security deposit (up to €1,200), repayable over 25 monthly instalments with no interest. Available to student employees or work-study students.

APL (or ALS) can be combined with some benefits: Mobili-Jeune (if you're a work-study student), a CROUS grant, and occasional local-authority support. However, you cannot combine APL and ALS: only one CAF benefit applies, depending on the type of home.
