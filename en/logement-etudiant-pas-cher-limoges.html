Cheap student housing in Limoges is not only possible — it's the norm

Good news for tight budgets: Limoges regularly ranks among the French student cities with the lowest cost of living, and housing is a big reason why. For a furnished studio of equivalent quality, you pay much less here than in the big cities.

In practice, with Begip, a student studio starts from €280/month including bills in the city centre, and from €350 in a serviced student residence. And all these rents are eligible for CAF benefits (APL or ALS), which cut the real cost even further. In other words: finding cheap student housing in Limoges isn't the exception — it's the norm, as long as you know where to look and which levers to pull.

The real lever

APL: what you actually pay each month

The advertised rent isn't the real rent. For a student, the APL (or ALS) from the CAF is deducted directly, and in Limoges it weighs heavily on the budget: on a studio at €350/month including bills, the aid is often between €100 and €180/month depending on your situation — bringing the real cost to around €170 to €250/month.

The exact amount depends on your income, the rent and your situation, and is calculated free of charge on the official CAF simulator. To understand everything (conditions, amounts, steps, applying), see our dedicated page on student housing benefits (APL/ALS) in Limoges.

Private or CROUS?

Begip studio or CROUS housing: which for a tight budget?

The CROUS offers the lowest rents in Limoges, but its homes are allocated first to grant holders, via the Student Social File, and are often full — with no guarantee of getting a place or choosing your area.

A private studio managed by Begip, on the other hand, is open to everyone, furnished, quickly available and already very affordable (from €280 bills included, APL deductible). It is the safest way to find cheap housing without depending on CROUS allocation. And if you're after a residence near the campuses, Begip also manages three student residences in Limoges from €350.
