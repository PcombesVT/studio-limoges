Being a prépa student in Limoges

Housing that helps you succeed in your prépa

A preparatory class is two intense years where independent study is everything. In that context, housing genuinely matters: a quiet studio to focus in the evenings and at weekends, and above all close to the school to avoid wasting time commuting and to be able to pop home between classes. In Limoges, the prépas are grouped in the city centre, which makes this choice very achievable.

Begip, a Limoges agency for over 35 years, manages many studios in this area. And because a prépa home is often decided as a family, you deal with an established agency and an identified contact — a clear lease, an inventory, ongoing management — rather than an anonymous private listing.

Budget & aid

How much is a studio for a prépa student?

In central Limoges, expect from around €280/month including charges for a furnished studio. It's the best-value area, and our homes are eligible for French housing benefit (APL or ALS): depending on the situation, it noticeably reduces the real rent.

For students, Begip charges a reduced agency fee of €130. Details and estimate on our page about student housing aid (APL/ALS) in Limoges.
