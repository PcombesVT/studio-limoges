The process

How to rent a studio
in Limoges with Begip

From your first enquiry to the handover of keys, here are the steps to rent your student studio in Limoges.

1
Contact Begip for availability
Call +33 5 55 33 97 50 or email agence@begip.fr. The team will tell you which studios are available based on your course, budget and arrival dates.
2
Visit the studio
Begip arranges viewings by appointment across all its homes. You can view several types to pick the size that suits you and your budget.
3
Put together your application
ID, proof of enrolment, guarantor proof (or a guarantee solution), income or a statement. The Begip team guides you so your application is complete first time.
4
Sign the lease and collect the keys
Once your application is accepted, you sign the lease and the check-in inventory at the agency. The fee (€130 all-in) is paid at this point. You're home!
5
Apply for your APL or ALS benefit
As soon as you move in, apply for CAF benefit at caf.fr. The Begip team can help you gather the documents. The allowance is paid from the following month.

In Limoges, a student studio rents for €280 to €500/month including charges, depending on size and district. Begip offers studios from €280/month (city-centre studio). With APL or ALS, the effective amount you pay can fall below €200/month for a student with no income of their own.
