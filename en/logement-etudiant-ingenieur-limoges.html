Being an engineering student in Limoges

Choosing housing to match your engineering school

In Limoges, the two main engineering schools are in two very different places — and that should guide your housing choice. 3IL (computer engineering) is right in the city centre: a central studio, everything on foot. ENSIL-ENSCI is at Ester Technopole, to the north-east, a little out of town: there, the key is being well connected (a bus line to Ester) while keeping access to shops and student life.

Begip, a Limoges agency for over 35 years, manages studios for both situations. And since the engineering schools of Limoges welcome many international students, we can help you find and book your studio before you even arrive in France.

Budget & aid

How much is a studio for an engineering student?

Expect from around €280/month including charges for a furnished studio. Our homes are eligible for French housing benefit (APL or ALS) — including for international students, subject to conditions. Depending on your situation, it can cover a significant share of the rent.

For students, Begip charges a reduced agency fee of €130. Details and estimate on our page about student housing aid (APL/ALS) in Limoges.

What ENSIL-ENSCI and 3IL students ask before choosing their studio.

Where should an engineering student live in Limoges?
It depends on your school. 3IL is central: a central studio is ideal, everything on foot. ENSIL-ENSCI is at Ester (NE): better a studio in town on a bus line to Ester. Begip offers studios for both, from €280/month. See studio rentals →
Is ENSIL-ENSCI far from the centre of Limoges?
It's at Ester Technopole, north-east, a little out of town, but served by bus. Many students prefer a studio in town on a line to Ester, to keep access to shops and student life.
Can you book a studio in Limoges from abroad?
Yes. The engineering schools welcome many international students. Begip can support you remotely: tell us your school and your dates, and we'll point you to the available studios before you arrive.
What engineering schools are there in Limoges?
Mainly ENSIL-ENSCI (generalist + ceramics, at Ester) and 3IL (computer engineering, in the centre). Both award a CTI-accredited engineering degree, with work-study possible. See all Limoges courses →
