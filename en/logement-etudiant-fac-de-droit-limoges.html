Studying law & economics in Limoges

Living near the Faculty of Law and Economics

The Faculty of Law and Economics of Limoges (5 rue Félix Eboué) is right in the city centre, a stone's throw from the town hall, the Bénédictins railway station and the main shopping streets. That's a real advantage: unlike the science or health campuses, here the best place to live is also the most central.

For a student in a law degree, in economics-management, in AES or in the IPAG's Public Administration degree, a city-centre studio means everything on foot: lectures in the morning, the library in the afternoon, shops and student life in the evening — no car, no transport pass. Begip, a Limoges agency for over 35 years, manages many studios in this exact area, often the most affordable in town.

The faculty at a glance

The Limoges Law & Economics School

Part of the University of Limoges, the faculty offers Bachelor's, Master's and Doctoral programmes in private law, public law, economics and management. It is well known for its Master's in International and Comparative Environmental Law, recognised across Europe, as well as its public-law tracks.

In the same area, the IPAG (Institute for General Administration Preparation) runs the Public Administration degree and prepares students for civil-service competitive exams. Economics-management students will also find the nearby IAE — University Business School.

📍 Address: Faculty of Law and Economics, 5 rue Félix Eboué, 87031 Limoges — city centre.

How much does a student studio near the Law School cost?
From around €280/month including charges in the centre. It's the best-value area, ideal for law, economics, AES or IPAG students, and eligible for French housing benefit (APL/ALS).
Do you need a car to study at the Law School?
No. As the faculty is in the very centre, a city-centre studio lets you do everything on foot: lectures, the IAE, shops, station and buses. A car is unnecessary day to day, which further reduces the budget.
Is there a student residence near the Law School?
Since the Law School is central, the best-suited option is an independent studio in the centre rather than a dedicated residence (Limoges student residences are mostly near the IUT / La Borie and health campuses). Begip manages many studios right in the centre, next to the faculty.
