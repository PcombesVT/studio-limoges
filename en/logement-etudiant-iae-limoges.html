Studying management in Limoges

Living near the IAE — University Business School

The IAE of Limoges (3 rue François Mitterrand) is right in the city centre, a stone's throw from the Law Faculty, the town hall and the Bénédictins station. It is one of the best student locations in town: here, the most convenient housing is also the most central.

For a student on a Bachelor in Management, a Master in Management, Marketing, Finance or Human Resources, a city-centre studio means everything on foot: classes, group work, shops and student life — no car, no transport pass. And for the IAE's many work-study students, it is ideal: close to school during class weeks, well connected to reach the company. Begip, a Limoges agency for over 35 years, manages many studios in this area — often the most affordable in town.

The school in brief

The IAE of Limoges, the university's management school

Part of the University of Limoges, the IAE (University Business School) trains for management and business careers. It offers a Bachelor in Management, vocational bachelor's degrees and many master's degrees: management, marketing and sales, finance and management control, human resources, project management, entrepreneurship, as well as the MAE (Master in Business Administration), open to non-management profiles.

Many of these programmes are offered as work-study (apprenticeship or professional contract) — the IAE is known for its ties with local companies. Students are next door to the Law and Economics Faculty, in the same city-centre area.

📍 Address: IAE of Limoges, 3 rue François Mitterrand, 87031 Limoges — city centre.

Budget & benefits

How much does a studio near the IAE cost?

In the city centre, expect from around €280/month including bills for a furnished studio. It is the best value-for-money area in Limoges. Our homes are eligible for CAF benefits (APL or ALS): depending on your situation, the benefit can cover a significant part of the rent.

Work-study students can also claim the Mobili-Jeune aid from Action Logement (part of the rent covered, combinable with APL). For students, Begip charges reduced agency fees of €130. To estimate your net cost, see our page on student housing benefits (APL/ALS) in Limoges.
