Sciences & Technology — West Limoges

Campus La Borie

The main science hub of Limoges, the La Borie campus hosts the Faculty of Sciences, the IUT, STAPS and several major preparatory classes. Our Meissonier and Dutreix residences are under a 15-min walk away — ideal for students in Sciences, IUT and STAPS.

University of Limoges
Faculty of Sciences & Technology
🔬

The Faculty of Sciences & Technology of Limoges offers bachelor's, master's and doctoral programmes across many fields: mathematics, computer science, physics, chemistry, biology, geology and Earth sciences. It is known for the quality of its science pathways and its closeness to the University's research laboratories.

📍 123 Avenue Albert Thomas, 87060 Limoges
📞 05 55 45 72 00
🎓 Bachelor · Master · Doctorate
University of Limoges
IUT du Limousin
🏭

The IUT du Limousin trains higher technicians and BUT (university technology bachelor's) graduates across many specialities: computing, civil engineering, business and administration management, networks and telecommunications. Known for its close ties with the regional economy, the IUT helps its graduates into work.

📍 Campus Maurois, 12 Allée André Maurois, 87065 Limoges
📞 05 55 43 43 55
🎓 BUT (3 yrs) · DUT
University of Limoges
STAPS — Sport Sciences
⚽

The STAPS programme in Limoges prepares students for careers in sport, physical education and coaching. Students enjoy quality sports facilities on the La Borie campus and teaching that combines theory (biomechanics, sport psychology, physiology) with intensive practice on the field.

📍 Campus La Borie — 123 Av. Albert Thomas, 87000 Limoges
📞 05 55 45 72 00
🎓 Bachelor · Master · Coaching · Sport management
Sixth-form — Preparatory classes
Lycée Suzanne Valadon — CPGE
📐

Lycée Suzanne Valadon runs preparatory classes for the grandes écoles (CPGE) in science streams (MPSI, PCSI, BCPST) and arts streams (Hypokhâgne/Khâgne). These two very intensive post-secondary years prepare students for the entrance exams of top engineering, veterinary and business schools.

📍 39 Rue François Perrin, 87000 Limoges
📞 05 55 45 56 00
🎓 Science & Arts prep classes (1–2 yrs)
Sixth-form — BTS
Lycée Turgot — BTS
💼

Lycée Turgot offers several BTS (higher technician) streams in the service and technical sectors. These two-year courses, leading to a two-year diploma, are valued for their direct link to work and their partnerships with local firms through apprenticeships.

📍 6 Rue Paul Derignac, 87000 Limoges
📞 05 55 12 31 00
🎓 BTS (2 yrs) — full-time and apprenticeship
Arts · Health · Art — South-west Limoges

Vanteaux Campus & Health Hub

The Vanteaux campus brings together arts, architecture and several health courses. The CHU and the paramedical institutes complete this in the Marcland area. The E-Magine residence in Isle is 10 minutes by bus from the Marcland campus — ideal for students in Medicine, Pharmacy and nursing (IFSI).

University of Limoges
Faculty of Medicine & Pharmacy
🩺

The Faculty of Medicine and Pharmacy of Limoges trains future doctors, pharmacists, midwives and health professionals. Attached to the CHU of Limoges, it offers an outstanding clinical learning environment. The PASS pathway and the LAS routes give access to health studies after school.

📍 Campus Marcland — 2 rue du Docteur Marcland, 87025 Limoges
🎓 PASS · Medicine · Pharmacy · Midwifery · Physiotherapy
University of Limoges
Faculty of Arts & Humanities
📚

The Faculty of Arts and Humanities of Limoges teaches students in languages, literature, history, geography, philosophy, psychology and education sciences. It is notably recognised for its master's in museology and its applied foreign languages (LEA) courses, highly valued by employers.

📍 39E rue Camille Guérin, 87000 Limoges
🎓 Bachelor · Master · Arts · Languages · History · Psychology
Art & Design school
ENSAD — National School of Art & Design
🎨

The National School of Art & Design of Limoges (formerly ENSA) is world-renowned for its training in ceramic design and object design — a direct legacy of the Limoges porcelain tradition. Over 5 years (DNA and DNSEP) it trains creative designers and high-level visual artists.

📍 Campus de Vanteaux, Avenue Martin Luther King, 87000 Limoges
🎓 DNA (3 yrs) · DNSEP (5 yrs) — Design & Art
Paramedical training
IFSI — CHU Nursing School
🏥

The nursing school (IFSI) of the CHU of Limoges prepares students for the State Nursing Diploma over 3 years. Training alternates theory and clinical placements within the CHU and partner health facilities. The diploma also carries bachelor's-degree status.

📍 39F rue Camille Guérin, 87000 Limoges
🎓 State Nursing Diploma — 3 yrs
Paramedical training
ILFOMER — Rehabilitation Professions Institute
🦴

ILFOMER brings together several institutes for the rehabilitation professions: physiotherapy, speech therapy, occupational therapy, podiatry and psychomotor therapy. These 3-to-5-year courses lead to sought-after paramedical careers, in private practice and in health facilities.

📍 39F rue Camille Guérin, 87000 Limoges
🎓 Physiotherapy · Speech therapy · Occupational therapy · Psychomotor therapy
University teaching hospital
CHU de Limoges
🏨

The University Hospital of Limoges is a regional reference centre that welcomes many students on placement each year: medical, pharmacy, nursing and physiotherapy students… The CHU is also a major medical-research centre linked to the University of Limoges.

📍 2 Avenue Martin Luther King, 87000 Limoges
🎓 Medical & paramedical placements — clinical training site
Law · Management · Engineering — City centre

Limoges city centre

Central Limoges brings together several courses in law, management, business and computing. Our city-centre studios, from €280/month, are a few minutes' walk from the Law Faculty, the IAE and the schools — the best value for student housing near the Law Faculty in Limoges, walkable from the Bénédictins station.

University of Limoges
Faculty of Law & Economics
⚖️

The Faculty of Law and Economics of Limoges is recognised for the excellence of its programmes in international public law and environmental law. It offers bachelor's, master's and a doctorate in private law, public law, economics and management. Its master's in international environmental law is respected across Europe.

📍 5 rue Félix Eboué, 87031 Limoges
🎓 Bachelor · Master · Doctorate — Law, Economics, Management

University Management School
IAE Limoges — University Management School
📊

The IAE of Limoges is the University of Limoges' management school. It teaches management sciences that combine academic rigour with a practical approach: a bachelor's in economics-management, and master's degrees in management, management control, HR, marketing and entrepreneurship.

📍 3 rue François Mitterrand, 87000 Limoges
🎓 Bachelor · Master — Management, Finance, HR, Marketing
Private engineering school
3IL — Ingénieur Limoges
💻

3IL is an engineering school specialising in computing and information systems, training general-purpose digital engineers over 5 years. Students can specialise in cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, software development or IT project management. The school is CTI-accredited and offers apprenticeship pathways.

📍 43 rue Sainte Anne, 87000 Limoges

Engineering & Innovation — North Limoges

Ester Technopole

Ester Technopole is the technology-excellence hub of Limoges, bringing together leading engineering schools, research labs and high-tech companies. Reachable by bus from the city centre.

Engineering school
ENSIL-ENSCI — Limoges Engineering School
⚙️

ENSIL-ENSCI (the national engineering school of Limoges and the national school of industrial ceramics) trains high-level engineers in electrical, mechanical, materials, industrial and ceramic engineering. Located at Ester Technopole, it benefits from a leading research and innovation environment.

📍 16 rue Atlantis, 87280 Limoges (Ester Technopole)
🎓 Engineering degree (5 yrs) — CTI-accredited · Apprenticeship
Technology innovation hub
Ester Technopole
🚀

Ester Technopole is not just a school but an innovation ecosystem unique in Nouvelle-Aquitaine. It brings together technology companies, research labs, ENSIL-ENSCI and several startup incubators. For students on apprenticeships or placements, Ester is an outstanding professional playground.

📍 1 avenue d'Ester, 87069 Limoges
🎓 Business & research hub — placements, apprenticeships, CIFRE PhDs
Business · Health · Design — South Limoges

Other schools & courses

Limoges is also home to several private and specialist schools in business, management, design and health professions, mainly in the Bobillot and Mas-Jambost areas.

Private school — Health
KEYCE Santé Limoges
💊

KEYCE Santé Limoges is part of the national KEYCE Academy network, specialising in health-profession training. The school offers BTS and post-secondary courses in optics, hearing-aid dispensing and orthotics. Classes alternate theory and clinical practice in partner practices and centres.

📍 59-61 rue Bobillot, 87100 Limoges
🎓 BTS Optics · Hearing-aid · Orthotics (2 yrs)
Private school — Business & Management
ECEMA — Business & Management School
🤝

ECEMA is a business and management school offering courses open after school, at bachelor's and master's level. The school places a strong emphasis on work readiness: students do long placements and can study through apprenticeships. Career paths include sales, marketing, human resources and business management.

📍 59-61 rue Bobillot, 87100 Limoges
🎓 Bachelor (3 yrs) · Master (5 yrs) — Business, Management
Design — Lycée Mas Jambost
Design DN Made Limoges
✏️

The DN MADE (national diploma in art and design professions), offered at Lycée Mas Jambost in Limoges, trains versatile creators over 3 years in graphic design, applied arts and craft professions. This three-year diploma draws on the industrial and artistic tradition of Limoges in material design.
