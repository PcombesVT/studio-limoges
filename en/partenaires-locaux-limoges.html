A local spirit

Local businesses, just steps from our studios

Housing students and working people in Limoges for over 35 years means knowing the city well — and its best spots. Many of our studios and one-room flats are right in the city centre, where neighbourhood life happens on foot: shops, restaurants, cafés.

We've chosen to highlight local partner businesses based close to our homes. The idea is simple: help you discover genuinely good local spots when you move in, and support local Limoges business. Here are our first partners.

Why partners ?

A good home isn't just its walls: it's lived in its surroundings. In central Limoges, everything is within walking distance — and our partners are part of that pleasant everyday life, just minutes from your door.
