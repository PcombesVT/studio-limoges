Housing benefit

Two possible benefits
depending on your home

Depending on the studio you rent, you may receive one of these CAF benefits:

🏛️ APL — personalised housing benefit
Applies to certain approved (conventionné) homes. The amount depends on your income and household.
🏠 ALS — social housing allowance
For non-approved homes. Open to all students, subject to income. Often equivalent to APL.

💼 The Begip team helps you put together your housing-benefit application. Don't hesitate to get in touch!

Apprentices and work-study students can also claim the Mobili-Jeune grant from Action Logement, on top of APL or ALS.

Limoges, a student city

Living well in Limoges
as a student

Limoges is a human-scale, affordable and lively city. Here's why thousands of students choose to settle here.

💰
Rents among the lowest in France
The average studio rent in Limoges is around €380 including charges — well below the big cities. Ideal for keeping your student budget under control.
🚌
Efficient public transport
Buses and trolleybuses serve every campus. The annual STCL student pass is very affordable. All our homes are under 5 minutes from a stop.
🍕
University canteens & shops
The Limoges CROUS runs 8 university canteens. The city centre is full of shops, bakeries, supermarkets and student-priced restaurants.
💶
Reduced student agency fee: €130
Begip charges a reduced student-only agency fee: just €130 for the whole application (including the inventory). A real saving on your moving-in budget.
🔧
Fast maintenance via Home Service
Begip works with Home Service (a multi-trade company) and skilled local tradespeople to step in quickly when needed: cisterns, locks, traps, door handles… Your comfort is our priority.
🎉
Nightlife & culture
Bars, concerts, festivals, cinemas, museums — Limoges offers a rich cultural life for a city of 200,000. Perfect after revision!
🌿
A pleasant place to live
Parks, the banks of the Vienne, quiet residential districts — Limoges offers real quality of life, away from big-city stress.
🎓
17,500 students, a real community
Student societies, sports clubs, inter-faculty events — the University of Limoges is a human-scale institution where it's good to study and live.
🚄
Limoges, very well connected
Reachable via the A20 motorway (Paris–Toulouse), by train from the beautiful Bénédictins station (Paris in about 3 hours), and by air via Limoges-Bellegarde international airport. Perfect for weekends back home!
🌲
Outstanding natural surroundings
Just outside Limoges lies beautiful countryside: forests, lakes, hiking trails, bike rides, trail running, water sports on Lake Vassivière… A rare setting for a university city.
