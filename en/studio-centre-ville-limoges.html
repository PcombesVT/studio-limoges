Why the city centre

Everything on foot, the best budget in Limoges

Living in the city centre means going without a car and long bus journeys: shops, restaurants, markets, libraries, gyms and student life are all a few minutes from home. And it is also the area with the gentlest rents in the city.

🚶 Everything within walking distance
🚆 Bénédictins station (Paris in 2h30)
💶 Studios from €280/month
✅ Eligible for APL / ALS
🎓 Reduced fees €130 for students

The centre of Limoges concentrates much of student life: it is where you find most of the cafés, music venues, cinemas and places to go out, but also the most affordable studios in the city. For a student in law, management, computer engineering or a prep class, no area is more convenient.

Schools within walking distance

Studying in the centre of Limoges

Several major faculties and schools are located right in the centre or a few minutes' walk away. Living in the centre means getting up later and coming home earlier.

University of Limoges
Faculty of Law & Economics

The FDSE, right in the centre (rue Félix Eboué), teaches law, economics and management. Renowned for its international environmental law. A city-centre studio is the most practical option for law students.

📍5 rue Félix Eboué — city centre
University Business School
IAE Limoges

The IAE (Institute of Business Administration), the university management school of Limoges, offers a bachelor's in economics and management and master's degrees in management, marketing, finance, HR, management control and entrepreneurship, right in the city centre. Its students favour a nearby studio, reachable on foot.

📍3 rue François Mitterrand — city centre
Engineering school
3IL — Ingénieur Limoges

3IL trains engineers in computer science and digital technology (5-year), right in the city centre (rue Sainte-Anne). A central studio is ideal for its students, many of whom study on a work-study basis.

📍43 rue Sainte-Anne — city centre
Preparatory classes (CPGE)
Prep schools — Gay-Lussac & Léonard-Limosin

The Gay-Lussac and Léonard-Limosin lycées host the preparatory classes for the grandes écoles (science, humanities, business), in the heart of Limoges. Many prep students look for a quiet studio nearby.

📍Centre & near the city centre
University of Limoges
IPAG — Institute of Public Administration

Attached to the Faculty of Law, the IPAG prepares students for public-service entrance exams. Like the FDSE, it sits in the city centre — a nearby studio is the most logical solution.

📍Turgot campus — city centre
Private post-bac schools
Business, IT & health schools

Limoges has several private schools (business, management, IT, health — ECEMA, KEYCE, etc.) and lycée BTS courses, with campuses in or near the centre. A city-centre studio conveniently serves them all.

Frequently asked questions

City-centre student studio: your questions

How much does a student studio cost in the centre of Limoges?
In the centre of Limoges, a student studio rents from around €280 per month including charges. It is the best-budget area in the city, ideal for students at the Law Faculty, IAE, 3IL and the central schools. These homes are eligible for CAF housing benefit (APL or ALS), which further reduces the amount you actually pay.
Which schools are within walking distance of the city centre?
From the city centre you can walk to the Faculty of Law and Economics, the IAE, the 3IL engineering school, the IPAG, the preparatory classes (Gay-Lussac, Léonard-Limosin) and several private schools. Living in the centre removes the commute for all these students.
City-centre studio or student residence in Limoges?
An independent city-centre studio offers autonomy, the best budget (from €280/month) and everything on foot. Limoges' student residences are mostly on the IUT / La Borie side; for students in law, management, computer engineering or prep classes, the city centre remains the most convenient area.
