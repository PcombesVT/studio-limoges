Aid & work-study budget

What housing aid can a work-study student get?

Good news: a work-study student can often combine two schemes. On one side, French housing benefit (APL or ALS), based on income. On the other, the Mobili-Jeune grant from Action Logement — €10 to €100/month, reserved for work-study students under 30 on a private-sector contract. The two combine and clearly lighten the rent.

For students and work-study students, Begip charges a reduced agency fee of €130. See the detail on our page about student housing aid (APL/ALS) in Limoges, and remember to apply for Mobili-Jeune as soon as you sign your contract.

Can a work-study student get housing benefit?
Yes: French housing benefit (APL/ALS) based on income, plus the Mobili-Jeune grant from Action Logement (€10 to €100/month) for work-study students under 30 in the private sector. The two combine.
Where are the work-study schools in Limoges?
The private schools (ECEMA, Groupe Alternance, KEYCE, Pigier…) are mostly on the Bobillot / east side of the city, and many work-study BTS programmes are offered in the city-centre high schools. A central studio, or one near these areas, makes the school ↔ company commute easy.
